Automotive and EV study in China after Grade 12

2027 intake planning for a Pakistani CBSE student living in Kuwait. Built from the research report dated 11 September 2026.

An applied bachelor is the best route

For this student, the best path is usually an applied bachelor degree. Not a highly theoretical mechanical engineering degree, and not a short workshop certificate either.

The strongest current fit is Chang’an University in Xi’an, especially Automobile Service Engineering or Intelligent Vehicle Engineering. The university officially lists both as English medium, tuition is low, and Xi’an has a major EV manufacturing cluster led by BYD.

Evidence rule used throughout: if an official source does not confirm a fact, it is marked as not verified.

    Why many good colleges are missing from the top

    China has many excellent automotive vocational colleges. Most do not publish clear proof that their automotive majors are open to international students in English. An English website is not proof of English teaching, so those programs are marked conditional here rather than application ready.

            The most important value finding

            Chang’an University is unusually strong because a verified English automotive bachelor costs only RMB 16,000 a year in tuition. Fuyao is the opposite: academically interesting, but full fee tuition is RMB 350,000 a year. Treat Fuyao as scholarship dependent.

            Scholarships

            Scholarship windows often close before self funded deadlines. Do not budget on any award until it is issued in writing.

                Why Xi’an deserves attention

                It is cheaper than Shanghai or Shenzhen, yet official city figures report NEV output above one million units in 2025 and a major BYD presence.

                Two other strong clusters

                Chongqing reported 2025 automobile output of 2.788 million units, including 1.296 million NEVs.

                Wuhan’s development zone reports a large network of vehicle makers and parts suppliers.

                China against six other countries

                Is China actually better?

                For this profile, China wins on total cost and direct exposure to the world’s largest EV manufacturing ecosystem, but only if the program is genuinely taught in English. Malaysia becomes very competitive if an English friendly practical setting matters most. Germany is stronger for classic automotive engineering if he learns German. Canada, Australia and the UK offer clearer English environments and stronger post study routes, at several times the cost.
